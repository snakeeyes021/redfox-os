The files are in `files/system/usr/share/backgrounds/redfox/`. A copy of these credits is in `CREDITS` there, and the artist and licence are also in the wallpaper's entry in `files/system/usr/share/gnome-background-properties/00-redfox.xml`.

### How they were identified

Neither file carries the photo's ID. Each was matched against Czerwiński's Unsplash photos by comparing small thumbnails pixel by pixel:
- The dark file is identical to `sxaYEsE12RM`.
- The light file is identical to the top 16:9 crop of `XQqd6JKDkSM`.

Both pages credit Paweł Czerwiński and say "Free to use under the Unsplash License".

## The licence, for a distro

The [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) lets us copy, modify and distribute the photos, including in a free OS, with no permission needed. It has two limits:
- The photos can't be sold "without significant modification". Bundling them as wallpapers in a free OS isn't selling them.
- They can't be compiled "to replicate a similar or competing service", meaning a stock-photo site.

Neither affects RedFoxOS.
